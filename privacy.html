This Privacy Notice for Deacon Lock (doing business as HelperSync) ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:
  • Visit our website at https://helper-sync.com or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice

It helps the employer build a personalised weekly schedule of household tasks (generated by AI from a voice-first onboarding moment), share that schedule with the helper in the helper's native language, track task completion via photo proof, and coordinate across multiple household members.

10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

11. DOMESTIC HELPER ACCOUNTS

Domestic Helper Accounts. HelperSync is used by both household employers and the foreign domestic helpers they employ. When an employer invites a helper to the App, the employer provides limited information about the helper (typically a first name) and grants the helper a PIN to access the App. HelperSync acts as a data processor for that information on behalf of the household. Helpers using the App may at any time request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held about them by contacting support@helper-sync.com, and we will work with both the helper and the household to honour the request. Helpers retain all rights afforded to them under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), and we will not share helper data with third parties (including helper agencies or employment agencies) without the helper's prior explicit consent.
